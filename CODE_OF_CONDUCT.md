# Code of conduct

SelectSpeak contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, welcome
constructive disagreement, and focus criticism on the work rather than the
person. Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of another person's
private information, and intentionally disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these
expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
necessary. Conduct concerns should be sent privately through the repository's
security-advisory contact path when a public discussion would expose personal
information. Maintainers will consider reports fairly and protect reporters'
privacy as far as practical.
